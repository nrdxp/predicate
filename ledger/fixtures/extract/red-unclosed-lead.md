# Red fixture — an assertive lead whose bold never closes

`signer:: agent/composer` · `at:: abc1234`

`[U1] grade::proved` This document carries a graded node, so it is under the
discipline and every claim in it is expected to state its backing.
`check:: true`

**Unclosed emphasis is still emphasis: this paragraph opens the record's
assertive form and never closes the pair, so a detector keyed to the closing
marks finds no lead here and drops the claim it was written to catch.

The paragraph above carries exactly one bold marker, and that is the whole
fixture: the property the detector is measured against is *the paragraph
opens with* the marker, never *the paragraph contains a closed pair*. A
writer who declines to type a claim is not thereby a careful typist, so the
malformed spelling of the assertive form is the spelling this evasion arrives
in most often — and it is the one spelling a pair-matching detector cannot
see.
