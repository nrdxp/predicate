`signer:: agent/composer` · `at:: 3f1c9a2`

This fixture is SYNTHETIC and is extracted together with xdoc-closer.md.
Nothing here reaches outward: every node is a target, and what the record
must show is which of them the other document reached and which it did not.

Q1 carries the same marker as a question in the closing document, and that
collision is the point. A resolution rule that widened a plain reference to
the whole corpus would close this one too, and the record would then report
a question answered that nobody answered.
