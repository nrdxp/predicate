asserts in the record's assertive form, opens no marker, and stands above

The order above is the one an evasion arrives in most easily. A writer opens
with the assertion they decline to type and grades further down, and every
other fixture beside this one places its untyped claim after the node, where
either reading of the scoping rule reports it.
